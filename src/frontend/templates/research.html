Three-Dimensional Time Model

FreightProspecting enforces three non-negotiable temporal dimensions across all evidence records:

1. source_published_at
Timestamp when source document/article was officially published by the company or authority.
2. event_at
Timestamp when the real-world event occurred. Nullable for persistent facts (addresses, phone numbers). No text strings like 'Active'.
3. observed_at
Timestamp when the researcher or system retrieved and recorded the evidence.
