What is the Evidence Ledger?

This is your research reference library: company websites, directories, public records, and other sources used to support company details. Open a source to check the original information before you call.

A source is a reference, not proof of every company detail. For the evidence behind a specific fact, open the company's Review Prospect page.

PRIMARY SOURCE

From the original organization

For example, a company's own website or an official record. It still needs to support the specific fact being checked.

SECONDARY SOURCE

From another publisher

For example, a directory or industry article. Use it as a lead and confirm important details where possible.

What do the dates mean?

Published

When the page or document was published, if known.

Event date

When the event happened. General company details may have no event date.

Checked

When the researcher recorded this source. It may have changed since then.
