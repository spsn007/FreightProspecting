In accordance with our zero-hallucination standard, the database contains zero fake or hypothetical truck postings. In freight brokerage prospecting, shippers and freight demand come first.

Core Real-World Entity Distinctions:
  • Carrier Directory ≠ Historical Lane Activity ≠ Capacity Posting: A registered trucking company profile in carriers is distinct from historical route patterns in carrier_lane_activity, and both are distinct from a specific truck available on a confirmed date in capacity_postings.
  • Specific Match Invariant: A valid match exists only between an actual confirmed load_opportunity and an actual capacity_posting. The application never matches a vague prospect to a general carrier directory entry.
  • Cross-Border Capability: Carriers are classified as US_ONLY, CANADA_ONLY, or US_CANADA_CAPABLE. A domestic-only carrier is never matched to a cross-border load.
