How to use this guide

1. Choose United States or Canada. 2. Search the company's state/province or city. 3. Check its local clock and the coloured status dot. The India line translates that office window into your time.

Some states and provinces span different time zones, so check the city in the region details. Unknown split-zone cities are labelled “Time zone needs confirmation” on company cards. A company can appear when one of its facilities matches; check the facility you are calling.

Clocks tick every second and sync with the server every minute. Returning to this tab refreshes them immediately. If a clock loses sync, its calling status is hidden until it reconnects. Future follow-up conversions use the rules for the selected date.
