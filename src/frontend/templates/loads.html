In accordance with our zero-hallucination standard, the database contains zero fake or speculative loads. Core principle: Shipper Prospect ≠ Freight-Demand Signal ≠ Discrete Load. A facility prospect exists independently of active freight demand.

How Freight Progresses in V1 (Entity Separation):
  1. 1. Shipper Prospect: A physical shipping facility (recorded in facilities) that exists independently of active loads.
  2. 2. Freight-Demand Signal: An observed indicator of shipping activity, such as a hiring spike, plant expansion, or logistics RFP (recorded in freight_demand_signals).
  3. 3. Discrete Load: An actual transportation requirement (recorded in load_opportunities) separated strictly into core shipment requirements vs commercial pricing details.
Core Shipment Details
Required to confirm load availability:
  • Verified Origin (City & State/Province)
  • Verified Destination (City & State/Province)
  • Equipment Category (Flatbed, Step Deck, Dry Van)
  • Pickup / Availability Window
Commercial Pricing Details (Rate-Dependent)
Rates are optional: A load legitimately exists even if pricing is incomplete.
  • Shipper Target Rate (recorded when known)
  • Carrier Target Cost (recorded when known)
  • Deterministic Margin Model: Gross margin is calculated deterministically when both rates are known. If either is missing, Margin is strictly UNKNOWN. Missing rates never invalidate a load.
