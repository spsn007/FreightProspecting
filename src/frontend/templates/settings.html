Operational Policies & Rules
Decision A: Geographic Scope
Lower 48 & Canada (Alaska Excluded)
Domestic US Lower 48 states, Canada (10 provinces & 3 territories), and cross-border lanes are active. Alaska is excluded by default in V1 unless explicitly toggled. Hawaii and Mexico are hard-excluded.
Equipment Scope
FTL Flatbed, Step Deck & Dry Van
Target equipment is Full Truckload (FTL) Flatbed, Step Deck, and Dry Van. Trailer length (48' vs 53') is an equipment/load capacity attribute, never a hard 53-ft restriction.
Decision B: Outreach Priority
Phone-First Prospecting
Verified direct phone lines and dock extensions are the highest-value sales assets. Valid email addresses remain supported and actionable for quotes and rate confirmations.
Decision H: Zero-Paid-AI Architecture
No Paid AI/API Required
Core application runs 100% locally on your machine without requiring Gemini, OpenAI, or Google Search API keys. Internet is only needed for external web research and public site links.
Suppression System
5 Scoped Suppression Levels
Prevents contacting restricted shippers or bidding on conflicting lanes across 5 scopes: COMPANY, FACILITY, COMPANY_LANE, FACILITY_LANE, and GLOBAL_LANE. Shippers with private fleets remain visible by default in prospecting views.
